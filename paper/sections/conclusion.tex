\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We set out to separate optimizer-state interventions from their effect on update scale in a setting
where the loss of learning ability is actually observed. The loss appears on both synthetic streams.
Replacing the optimizer state with a fresh one does not recover it. The only correctly corrected
intervention with a consistent positive difference does not exceed its update-norm control by our
minimum interesting effect. The largest gains come from mis-corrected arms that take much larger steps.
On this evidence we pause work on optimizer-state repair as a method in this setting. The draft is
offered as a careful diagnostic of one small setting, with its algebra and controls made explicit, and
not as evidence about optimizer-state repair in general.
