\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Loss of plasticity.}
\citet{dohare2024loss} document a loss of learning ability across many tasks. They report that Adam with
default $\beta$ values worsens it relative to SGD, and they track dead units, weight magnitude and
effective rank. \citet{lyle2023understanding} show that such diagnostics change the sign of their
correlation with plasticity across settings, and they relate plasticity loss to curvature.
\citet{lyle2024disentangling} separate pre-activation shift from parameter-norm growth.
\citet{wang2026predicting} predict trainability from gradient statistics rather than rank measures.
Their probe discards the Adam state, and no optimizer statistic is among their predictors. Remedies that
act on the weights include regenerative regularisation \citep{kumar2024maintaining}, soft parameter
resets \citep{galashov2024nonstationary} and paired fast/slow networks \citep{lee2024slow}.
\citet{klein2024plasticity} survey the reinforcement-learning literature.

\paragraph{Optimizer state under non-stationarity.}
\citet{lyle2023understanding} attribute large post-shift updates to $\hat m$ adapting faster than
$\hat v$, and mitigate them with a larger $\eps$ and a smaller $\beta_2$. In a toy reinforcement-learning
setting they report that resetting only the optimizer state did not improve plasticity.
\citet{dohare2024loss} use $\beta_1=\beta_2$ in reinforcement learning. \citet{lyle2024disentangling}
show one example in which resetting Adam before a new task reduces dead units.
\citet{asadi2023resetting} reset $m$, $v$ and the counter at every target-network update and find the
second moment responsible for most of the gain. \citet{ellis2024adam} reset only the counter and derive
the post-shift update magnitude with and without this reset. They report that a full reset was worse
than plain Adam in their DQN experiments, and they relate their method to learning-rate schedules.
\citet{kang2024continual} study resetting, carrying and averaging $m$ and $v$ separately, with forgetting
as the focus. \citet{hernandezgarcia2026scale} reset the optimizer and restart warm-up as a
\emph{control} to separate weight-level plasticity loss from stale state. \citet{sahu2026adapt} bound
the tracking error of Adam and SGD under drift.

\paragraph{Position of this draft.}
Everything above is prior work: the observation that optimizer state matters, the individual reset
variants, and the algebra of update magnitude after a gradient shift. Within the sources we checked, we
did not find a paired comparison of these variants against per-step update-norm controls on a probe task
with an observed loss of learning ability. That comparison is the only thing this draft adds. It is a
small diagnostic, not a new method.
