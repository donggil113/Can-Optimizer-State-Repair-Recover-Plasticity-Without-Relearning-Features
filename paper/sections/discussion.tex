\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What the observations support.}
In our setting there is a clear loss of learning ability on a comparable new task. A state-only
intervention with exact bias correction did not produce a benefit that survives matching its update
scale. The counter-only reset reduces, in the $\eps=0$ idealisation, to a learning-rate schedule. The
largest improvements came from arms that take much larger steps. Taken together, these point toward
update scale, not stale optimizer statistics, as the quantity to control first when asking whether
state repair helps. We regard this as a hypothesis for further testing, not as a conclusion.

\paragraph{What they do not support.}
We do not claim that optimizer-state repair cannot restore plasticity, that the \texttt{reset\_m}
residual is zero, or that the artifact arm's gain is entirely due to its learning rate. Non-significance
at three seeds is not equivalence. We also make no claim about frozen or preserved features: every
branch updates its weights, and CKA and parameter distance are descriptive only.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Oracle timing.} Every intervention is applied at a pre-specified task boundary. Nothing here
evaluates a boundary-free or online trigger.
\item \textbf{Objective mismatch.} Learning rates were tuned for online accuracy over the whole stream,
while the probe metric is the learning speed on one new task from a late checkpoint. A learning rate
tuned for the probe could shrink or remove the gap.
\item \textbf{One probe task per condition and two synthetic conditions.} Tasks, checkpoints and arms
are not independent replicates; the unit of replication is the seed.
\item \textbf{Three previously inspected seeds.} Seeds 3000--3002 were used in an earlier smoke run, so
this study is exploratory.
\item \textbf{Small scale, no real data.} A 32-unit MLP trained in pure Python. We used no image or text
benchmark and no pretrained weights.
\item \textbf{Controls not run.} There is no learning-rate-matched control for the artifact arms, no
probe-specific learning-rate tuning, and no coordinate-level traces (Appendix~\ref{app:planned}).
\item \textbf{$\eps$.} The scalar equivalences of Section~\ref{sec:analysis} hold only with $\eps=0$.
Their accuracy at $\eps=10^{-8}$ is checked only at the level of per-tensor norms in our logs.
\end{itemize}
